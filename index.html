Sky Scribe

An interactive sonification of an imaginary sky. Every star binds to two parameters of a generative synthesizer — its horizontal position governs one, its vertical position the other. Drag stars across the canvas to reshape the sound they control.

Hold Shift and click two stars to forge a constellation: the line that joins them couples their parameters into the rest of the patch, introducing harmonic chaos. The wheel anchored to the horizon is both the day-night cycle and the master volume — sun overhead is silence, moon overhead is full audio. Drag the sun or moon to spin it, or enable time below to let it rotate on its own.

4-minute cycle · 2 min day · 2 min night
